切 SLAM 时

只加 odom_topic，三条必须同一个值（MAVROS 时不用传，默认就是 /mavros/gps_input/

ros2 launch workspace_nav nav2_boat.launch.py use_rviz:=false enable_mission_brlocalization
ros2 launch dock_mission dock_mission.launch.py odom_topic:=/slam/localization
ros2 launch usv_docking docking.launch.py odom_topic:=/slam/localization

切回 MAVROS 时删掉 odom_topic:=... 即可。
